Research

FADA: Few-Shot Domain Adaptation via Dynamics Alignment for Humanoid Control

Angchen Xie*, Nikhil Sobanbabu*, Ishayu Shikhare, Alan Wang, Max Simchowitz, Guanya Shi* equal contribution

Under review

TL;DR: A three-stage Planner-IDM framework for humanoid control: freezing the planner and finetuning only the inverse dynamics model on about two minutes of target-domain rollouts aligns it to new terrain, payload, or actuator dynamics — no rewards or demonstrations needed.

arXiv Website Twitter

TACTIC: Tactile and Vision Conditioned Contact-Centric Control for Whole-Arm Manipulation

Rishabh Madan, Angchen Xie, Samantha Saak, Andres Blanco, Dohyeok Lee, Sarah Grace Brown, Yunting Yan, Mark Zolotas, Jose Barreiros, Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee

RSS 2026

TL;DR: Predicts how whole-arm contacts and forces will evolve by coupling learned latent dynamics with analytical kinematics through contact Jacobians, then plans with contact-aware MPC that trades task progress against force regulation.

arXiv Website Twitter

RIO: Flexible Real-Time Robot I/O for Cross-Embodiment Robot Learning

Pablo Ortega-Kral*, Eliot Xing*, Arthur Bucker, Vernon Luk, Junseo Kim, Owen Kwon, Angchen Xie, Nikhil Sobanbabu, Yifu Yuan, Megan Lee, Deepam Ameria, Bhaswanth Ayapilla, Jaycie Bussell, Guanya Shi, Jonathan Francis, Jean Oh* equal contribution

RSS 2026

TL;DR: An open-source Python framework that lets robots, sensors, teleoperation interfaces, and policies be swapped independently, validated on VLA deployment across three morphologies and four hardware platforms.

arXiv Website Code

PrioriTouch: Adapting to User Contact Preferences for Whole-Arm Physical Human-Robot Interaction

Rishabh Madan, Jiawei Lin, Mahika Goel, Angchen Xie, Xiaoyu Liang, Marcus Lee, Justin Guo, Pranav N. Thakkar, Rohan Banerjee, Jose Barreiros, Kate Tsui, Tom Silver, Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee

CoRL 2025

TL;DR: When multiple contacts impose conflicting force preferences no controller can satisfy all of them, so PrioriTouch learns to rank the competing objectives and executes them through hierarchical operational space control.

arXiv Website

Non-repetitive: A Promising LiDAR Scanning Pattern

Angchen Xie, Yeqiang Qian, Weihao Yan, Chunxiang Wang, Ming Yang

IROS 2024

TL;DR: Finds non-repetitive LiDAR the more promising scanning pattern, on the strength of its superior object scanning, using Repetitive-or-not — a CARLA dataset that captures both patterns simultaneously and exposes the domain gap between them.

IEEE Xplore Dataset
